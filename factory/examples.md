# What "good" looks like (the judge compares against this)

- The existing overview: dark sky, glowing Sun at true size, thin orbit lines, an asteroid belt of fine
  particles, small labels in spaced capitals. Calm, elegant, not cluttered.
- The planet close-up: a sphere wrapped in a shell of fine particles sampled from the planet's real colours;
  the open palm scatters them, the fist gathers them.
- Info panel: eyebrow (type), big title in the body's colour, 1–2 sentences, a 2-column grid of facts.

A new level should feel like the same app: fine additive particles with real colours (galaxy cores warm
yellow-white, arms blue-white with pink HII regions, nebulae red/teal), slow rotation, a clear focal object,
and enough empty space. Avoid big flat squares (point sizes too large), solid blobs, neon rainbow colours,
and text overlapping objects.
