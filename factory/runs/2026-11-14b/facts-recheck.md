- NASA Imagine (https://imagine.gsfc.nasa.gov/features/cosmic/local_group_info.html): "spread over a diameter of nearly 10 million
  light years"; "M31 and the Milky Way are the most massive members". ✔ Note: this page says "over 30 galaxies".
- NASA Science large-scale structures (https://science.nasa.gov/universe/galaxies/large-scale-structures/): "live alongside over 50
  other galaxies within the Local Group." ✔ (we cite this page for "more than 50")
- NASA Hubble M31: "2.5 million light-years" ✔. NASA Hubble M33: "About 3 million light-years"; "third-largest member of our Local
  Group ... following the Andromeda galaxy (M31) and the Milky Way" ✔ (supports Triangulum in "Biggest members").
- ESA Hubble & Gaia 2025: "approximately a 50-50 chance of the two galaxies colliding within the next 10 billion years." ✔
